THREE COWS - tested, and what each one honestly produces
========================================================

Every number below was produced by posting these exact files to the running
server. Nothing here is estimated.

FIRST, THE NUMBER YOU ASKED ABOUT. "Most of the twenty traits" is not
reachable and no photograph will change that. Twelve of the twenty need udder,
teat or rib landmarks that were never annotated - they return 0.0% on every
photograph ever tested, because the keypoint model was trained on 22 of 41
joints. The honest ceiling is 5 to 8. These three sets get 3, 4 and 3.


HOW TO USE A SET
----------------
Gallery button on each capture screen. Load side.jpg for the side step,
rear.jpg for the rear step, walking-video.mp4 for the video step, and tag.jpg
for the ear tag - then type that cow's animal id when the app asks.

     Udder Depth       score 3    15.7 cm      [rear]
  species  BUFFALO 1.000   group buffalo 1.000   consistent with the record

  THE STRONGEST THING IN THIS FOLDER: the classifier calls this animal a
  buffalo at full confidence, against a buffalo record. Species and group are
  the part that works with no tag and no scale at all.

  DO NOT QUOTE THE WEIGHT. It reads 1017-1474 kg. A real Murrah is 450-700.
  This buffalo wears her own yellow ear tag in the side photograph, the scale
  transfer latched onto it, and the assumed panel height is wrong - so the
  cm/px is wrong, and weight goes as the CUBE of that error. It is the exact
  failure documented in AUDIT-measured-or-invented.md section 2.

  If a judge spots it, that is a good moment, not a bad one:
    "That weight is wrong and we can tell you why. It's derived from the ear
     tag, the tag's true size is assumed rather than known, and weight goes as
     the cube of that error. It's why the weight card shows a range and a
     second method that disagrees rather than one confident number."

  IF YOU WOULD RATHER NOT SHOW A WRONG WEIGHT: skip the tag step. Without
  tag.jpg this set scores 1 trait (Rear Legs Set, score 9) and reports NO
  weight at all. Fewer traits, nothing to defend. Your call.

  rear.jpg is also a different animal - the stock rear view.

walking-video.mp4
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
  640x480, 85 frames, 12 fps, 7.1 seconds. A real Holstein really walking,
  side-on, across a farm yard.

  HONEST: 14 of those 85 frames are real. The source dataset keeps only every
  third frame, so the real footage covers about 1.4 seconds; the rest were
  interpolated with optical flow to make it watchable. The GAIT is real, the
  SPEED is roughly 5x slow motion.

  It changes no result. That was verified rather than assumed: the same
  session posted with and without the video returned identical scores, and the
  only difference anywhere in the response was captured.gait_video true vs
  false. Nothing in this build analyses gait. The clip exists so the capture
  step shows something real instead of a black screen.
