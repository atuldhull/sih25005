# When an animal can be scored

## The eligibility window
NDDB's guideline allows type classification only for females in their FIRST
lactation, between 30 and 90 days after calving, with a milk record on file.
Outside this window the app refuses to score and explains why - that refusal
follows the official rulebook, it is not a malfunction.

## Why the window exists
Body shape changes through life and through lactation. Scoring everyone at
the same biological stage (first lactation, early-mid) makes scores
comparable across animals and farms, which is what the breeding program
needs. Scoring a dry cow or a fifth-lactation cow would produce numbers that
cannot be compared fairly.

## Why udder photos have a preferred time
The guideline prefers udder assessment about one hour before evening milking,
when the udder is comfortably full. An empty udder hangs differently and
changes Udder Depth and attachment scores. If the same animal is photographed
at the wrong hour, her udder scores can shift - so the app records capture
time with every session.
