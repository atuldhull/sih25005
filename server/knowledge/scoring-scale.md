# The 1-9 scoring scale

## How the 1-9 scale works
Every trait is scored 1 to 9 on the ICAR linear scale. 1 and 9 are opposite
biological extremes, NOT bad and good. For Stature, 1 means very short and 9
means very tall - neither is better. Whether a score is desirable depends on
the breed and the breeding goal, which is decided later by the breeding
program. The app never colors scores as good or bad for this reason.

## Why some traits are not scored
The system refuses to score a trait when it is not confident - for example
when the body part is not clearly visible in the photo. A refused trait shows
the reason and how to fix it (usually retaking a photo). Refusing to score is
intentional: an honest blank is more useful to the breeding program than a
guessed number.

## What the confidence value means
Each score carries a confidence between 0 and 1. Angle-based traits measured
from keypoints usually have the highest confidence. The eight traits that
NDDB itself calls subjective (like teat placements and foot angle) naturally
carry wider uncertainty.
